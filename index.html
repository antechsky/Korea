Why these places are worth it

🏯 Gyeongbokgung
1395 · first and largest Joseon royal palace. Best for “this is Seoul” scale and ceremonial architecture.
🌿 Changdeokgung
1405 · UNESCO site · royal residence for roughly 270 years. Best for hanbok + natural landscape + Secret Garden.
🏘️ Bukchon
Around 600 years of urban history; traditional hanok neighborhood between palaces. Best for old-Seoul street atmosphere.
🏛️ Deoksugung
A palace tied to the late Joseon / Korean Empire period, notable for Korean and Western architecture standing together.
🌃 DDP
Zaha Hadid’s futuristic landmark. Best contrast after the royal-history days.
🌊 Han River
Not a monument — it is everyday Seoul. Picnic, ramen, sunset and skyline are part of the actual city experience.
