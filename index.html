Must-do / shopping cheat sheet

👘 Hanbok
Hair → rental → palace → Bukchon. Для classy фото брать classic/pastel, не гигантскую princess skirt.
💄 Beauty
Olive Young: sunscreen, lip tints, masks, hair/scalp care. Myeongdong — удобно для first/last run.
👗 Fashion
Seongsu — trend/pop-ups; Hongdae — younger streetwear; Apgujeong/Dosan — designer; The Hyundai — polished department-store edit.
📸 Photo booth
Сделайте один matching photo booth strip втроём в Hongdae/Seongsu.
🍜 Food
Samgyeopsal, dakgalbi, tteokbokki, mandu, bindaetteok, hotteok, Korean fried chicken, Han River ramen.
🚇 Transport
T-money + Naver Map/KakaoMap удобнее локально. Google Maps links здесь — быстрый handoff между точками.
